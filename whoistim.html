about
I am a tech enthusiast since birth. My father used to send PCs from abroad and I was immediately drawn to these machines. I grew up installing third-party games and learned to understand mobile developer modifications by trial and error.

My family was not always able to afford powerful hardware, so I was forced to deal with what I had — pushing myself to get the most out of the machines available to me.

I love machines and the power and control they give you.
